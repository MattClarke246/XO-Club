# Risk Register (Launch + Maintenance Model)

| Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Multi-state raffle compliance delay | High | Medium | Engage legal review before campaign launch | Client + Provider |
| Profit calculation dispute | High | Medium | Signed formula and cost inclusion schedule in addendum | Client + Provider |
| Misconfigured Shopify checkout env | Medium | Medium | Pre-launch hardening checklist + smoke test | Provider |
| Over-reliance on one raffle app | Medium | Medium | Maintain backup app shortlist and migration notes | Provider |
| Credential/access drift | High | Medium | Monthly access review and least-privilege roles | Client |
| Delayed monthly payout | Medium | Medium | Fixed report cadence and signed payout SLA | Client |
