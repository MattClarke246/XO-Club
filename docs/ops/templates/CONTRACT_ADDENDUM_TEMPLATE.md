# Contract Addendum Template (Commercial + Maintenance Share)

This template is a starting point only and is not legal advice. Use counsel review before signing.

## Parties

- Provider: `<your_name_or_company>`
- Client: `<client_name_or_company>`
- Effective Date: `<yyyy-mm-dd>`
- Existing Agreement Reference: `<msa_or_sow_reference>`

## 1) Commercial Terms

- One-time delivery fee: `$1000` (USD)
- Ongoing maintenance share: `15%` of monthly **Net Profit Base**
- Reporting period: monthly calendar period
- Payout due date: `<day_5_to_day_10_of_following_month>`

## 2) Net Profit Base Definition (required)

Net Profit Base =

1. Gross sales
2. minus discounts/promotions
3. minus refunds/returns
4. minus payment processor/transaction fees
5. minus shipping label/fulfillment shipping costs (merchant-paid only)
6. minus agreed recurring costs (apps, SaaS, ad spend, etc.) listed in Schedule A

Provider monthly maintenance payout = `Net Profit Base * 0.15`

## 3) Data Access and Reporting Rights

- Client grants Provider read-only access to required Shopify Analytics/Finance reports.
- Client grants Provider access to monthly exports needed to compute Net Profit Base.
- Source of truth reports are listed in Schedule B (report names + export format).

## 4) Change Control

- Any change to cost categories, payout formula, or payout timing requires written approval by both parties.

## 5) Security and Access

- Client owns all platform accounts and payment relationships.
- Provider receives least-privilege collaborator access only.
- Offboarding timeline after termination: `<number_of_days>`.

## 6) Termination

- Either party may terminate with `<notice_period>` written notice.
- Final month payout remains due per this addendum.

## 7) Signatures

- Provider signature/date
- Client signature/date
